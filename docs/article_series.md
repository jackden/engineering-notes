# AI-assisted Engineering Series

This series develops one argument across seven articles: AI-assisted engineering needs explicit workflow governance, evidence, and completion boundaries rather than increasingly long prompts or context windows.

## Reading order

1. **A01 — Prompt Is Not a Workflow Engine** (2026-05-13)
2. **A02 — Producing Work Is Not Completing Workflow** (2026-05-23)
3. **A03 — AI Can Produce Work, But What Counts as Evidence?** (2026-05-30)
4. **A04 — The Most Overlooked Problem in AI-Assisted Engineering** (2026-06-06)
5. **A05 — Completion Needs Evidence, Not Confidence** (2026-06-14)
6. **A06 — A Healthy Workflow Sometimes Says No** (2026-06-27)
7. **A07 — AI-Assisted Engineering Needs More Than Long Context** (2026-07-24)

## Argument progression

```text
A01  Prompt ≠ Workflow
       ↓
A02  Work ≠ Workflow Completion
       ↓
A03  Workflow must have Evidence
       ↓
A04  AI-assisted Engineering needs Workflow Governance
       ↓
A05  Completion must be supported by Evidence
       ↓
A06  Insufficient Evidence should make the Workflow say No
       ↓
A07  Long Context cannot replace accumulated Engineering Understanding
```

The first six articles establish the workflow and evidence boundary. A07 extends the argument to long-context systems, correction history, runtime enforcement, and the accumulation of engineering understanding across sessions.
