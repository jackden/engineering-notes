# Writing and Publication Guidelines

## Source language

English is the source language for the series. Keep established technical terms in English and explain them on first use when the surrounding prose benefits from a short gloss.

## Evidence boundary

Separate what the article observes, what it infers, and what remains unproven. Do not turn an adjacent paper or an illustrative figure into evidence for a stronger claim than its source supports.

## Publication unit

Each article has one canonical Markdown body, one metadata record, and article-owned figures. The series ID (`A01`, `A02`, …) is stable across the repository, GitHub Pages, PDFs, figures, and external publication references.

## Future translations

When translations are added, English remains the source. A translation follows this sequence:

```text
English source → AI-assisted translation → human review → Traditional Chinese publication
```

Translation terminology should follow `references/terminology.yaml`. Do not mark a translation as published until it has passed human review.
