# About Engineering Notes

Engineering Notes is a repository-native publication collection about building reliable engineering workflows around AI-assisted development.

The editorial focus is deliberately narrower than general AI productivity: generated work, evidence, validation scope, review freshness, workflow state, and the conditions under which a completion claim should be accepted.

## How to read this repository

Open an article directory and read its `README.md`. The neighboring `metadata.yaml` records publication metadata and the `figures/` directory contains article-owned visual assets.

## Content boundary

The published articles preserve their canonical wording. Unknown dates, incomplete platform metadata, and limits on data-backed visual claims remain marked as unknown or partial. Publication assets and provenance are kept separate from private editorial records.
